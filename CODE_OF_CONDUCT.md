# Code of Conduct

WP24Horas open-source spaces are for constructive technical collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code or decisions.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Assume good intent while still documenting technical risks clearly.
- Keep discussions relevant to the repository and its users.
- Respect privacy and do not expose personal, client or confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, spam, deliberate disruption, or publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project.

Repository-specific codes of conduct may define additional expectations and take precedence when present.
